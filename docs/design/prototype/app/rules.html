Alert rules

The rules that drive every alert: scope, metric, threshold, how long it must hold, severity, owner and escalation. New rules are backtested on today's synthetic data before they are created.

Why this matters Zone profile v12

Rules

Every alert on the operations screen comes from one of these rules. Rules covering zones outside this role are not listed.

How a rule fires

  • The rule is checked every simulated minute for each queue or sensor in its scope.
  • It fires when the condition has held for the "sustained for" minutes, and clears when the condition has been false for 5 minutes (the seeded nowcast rules clear below 12 minutes).
  • An open alert escalates to the named role if nobody acknowledges it in time. Acknowledgements are written to the audit log.
  • Email channels are recorded but not sent in the demo.
