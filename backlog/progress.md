## 2026-10-02 ARV-024 Declarative mapper and MQTT transport
- Summary: the `Declarative` dialect reads a vendor's JSON through a mapping shipped with Ariva (Ariva.Infra/Sensing/Mappings, embedded and checked at start-up, listed at `GET api/v1/admin/devices/mappings`); a device names it (`mappingName`, script 0015 with a check that ties it to the dialect); `POST api/v1/ingest/zones/{zone}/declarative`.
